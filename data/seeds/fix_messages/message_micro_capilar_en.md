*What is scalp micropigmentation and how does it work?*
*Scalp micropigmentation* is a non-invasive aesthetic technique that simulates the appearance of shaved hair or increased density in areas with hair loss. It is performed by implanting pigments into the epidermis of the scalp, creating the visual effect of hair follicles.
This procedure is ideal for men and women who want to conceal baldness, improve hair density in sparse areas, or cover scars on the scalp.

How does it work?
1. *Preliminary evaluation:* The patient's skin type, degree of alopecia, and needs are analyzed.
2. *Pigment Design and Selection:* A suitable shade is chosen so that the result is natural and harmonizes with the existing hair.
3. *Pigment Application:* Using a specialized dermographer, tiny dots of pigment are deposited into the skin, mimicking the appearance of follicles.
4. *Sessions and Touch-ups:* Typically, 2 to 3 sessions are required to achieve an optimal and lasting result.

Scalp micropigmentation is not a tattoo, as the pigments are designed to fade gradually over time, allowing for adjustments according to the patient's needs.

*Scalp Micropigmentation for Scars*
One of the most effective uses of scalp micropigmentation is to *cover scars on the scalp*. It can be used to conceal scars caused by:
- Hair transplants (FUE or FUT).
- Accidents or surgeries.

- Medical conditions that have left visible marks on the skin.

Benefits for scars
✅ *Natural effect:* The technique mimics hair growth in the affected area.
✅ *Improves aesthetics:* Reduces the visibility of scars, restoring the uniformity of the scalp.
✅ *Safe procedure:* It is a non-surgical method, with a quick recovery and minimal discomfort.
